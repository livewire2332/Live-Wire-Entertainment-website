⚡ About Live Wire

Live Wire Entertainment is all about bringing people together through music, laughter, positivity and a shared love of great tunes.

Welcome to Live Wire

Good Music • Good Vibes • Great People

From the Live Wire Bar to our live shows, the aim is simple: create a fun, friendly place where people can enjoy the music, have a laugh and feel part of the Live Wire family.

🛡️ Our Community

Live Wire is intended to be a positive, happy and safe community for enjoying tunes and company.

  • Everyone is welcome.
  • Respect and kindness come first.
  • No bullying, drama or harassment.
  • Keep microphones muted while joining guest boxes.
  • Keep alcohol off-screen.
  • Music requests are made through the current DJ's bio link.
