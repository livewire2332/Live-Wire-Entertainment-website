⚡ Live Wire Flagship Shows

At the heart of Live Wire Entertainment are two long-standing regular weekend shows — bringing music, nostalgia, banter and the Live Wire community together week after week.

🎧 Friday nights

Feel Good Friday with DJ Disco Dan

🕢 Fridays from 7:30pm

Your weekend starts here! Feel Good Friday is one of Live Wire Entertainment's flagship regular shows, hosted by DJ Disco Dan from the Live Wire Bar.

Expect music spanning the decades, big singalong favourites, forgotten gems, nostalgia, banter and plenty of feel-good energy. It's a proper Friday-night mix designed to get the weekend started.

  • 🎶 Music spanning the decades
  • 💿 Forgotten gems and music nostalgia
  • 🍹 Live Wire Bar atmosphere
  • 🔥 Regular Friday features and plenty of banter
🚂 Saturday nights

The Music Train with Mr Phoenix

🕢 Saturdays from 7:30pm

Next stop... The Music Train! Mr Phoenix brings Live Wire's Saturday flagship show, taking the community on another musical journey through the decades.

Expect big tunes, nostalgia, banter and plenty of surprises as the Music Train rolls into another Saturday night. And then there's Mr Phoenix himself — wicked, always ready to wind someone up in the guest boxes, armed with plenty of wit and cheeky charm, and never far away from a Nickelback reference. 😂🔥

It's all part of what makes the Music Train what it is: great music, plenty of laughs, a bit of mischief and the unmistakable Mr Phoenix personality.

  • 🚂 A musical journey through the decades
  • 🎵 Classic favourites and memorable tunes
  • 😂 Live Wire banter, guest-box winding-up and cheeky charm
  • 🎸 A healthy love of Nickelback
