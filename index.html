These are the two main Live Wire weekend shows — the nights that bring the biggest mix of music, banter, nostalgia and Live Wire vibes.

🎧 Friday nights

Feel Good Friday with DJ Disco Dan

🕢 Fridays from 7:30pm

Your weekend starts here! Music spanning the decades, big singalong favourites, forgotten gems, nostalgia, banter and plenty of feel-good energy from DJ Disco Dan at the Live Wire Bar.

  • 🎶 Music spanning the decades
  • 💿 Forgotten gems and nostalgia

🚂 Saturday nights

The Music Train with Mr Phoenix

🕗 Saturdays from 8pm

Next stop... The Music Train! Mr Phoenix brings Live Wire's Saturday flagship show, taking the community on another musical journey through the decades.

Expect big tunes, nostalgia, banter and plenty of surprises as the Music Train rolls into another Saturday night. And then there's Mr Phoenix himself — wicked, always ready to wind someone up in the guest boxes, armed with plenty of wit and cheeky charm, and never far away from a Nickelback reference. 😂🔥

  • 🚂 A musical journey through the decades
  • 🎵 Classic favourites and memorable tunes
  • 😂 Live Wire banter, guest-box winding-up and cheeky charm
  • 🎸 A healthy love of Nickelback
