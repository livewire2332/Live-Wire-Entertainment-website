🎧 When The Shows Are Live

Four nights, four very different flavours of Live Wire. Pick your night, pull up a bar stool and come and join the fun. 🍹⚡

📡 Wednesday nights

⚡ The Wednesday Wire with DJ Disco Dan

🕗 Live from 8pm

A proper midweek Live Wire catch-up packed with plenty of chat, great tunes and the perfect excuse to forget it's only Wednesday! 🎶😂

And who's behind the mic? The perfect chatty man on air, DJ Disco Dan, bringing the conversation, the music and a whole load of midweek energy into the Live Wire Bar.

🎁 Thursday nights

🎁 Surprise Package Thursdays with Mr Phoenix

🕗 Live from 8pm

Thursday comes with a musical mystery! Tune in with Mr Phoenix and find out exactly what surprise he's got packed into this week's Surprise Package. 🎶🔥

You never quite know what's coming next — but you can guarantee tunes, banter, a few surprises and probably a bit of Phoenix mischief along the way. 😂

🎧 Friday nights

🎧 Feel Good Friday with DJ Disco Dan

🕢 Live from 7:30pm

Your weekend starts here! DJ Disco Dan takes over the Live Wire Bar with music spanning the decades, forgotten gems, big singalong moments and enough feel-good energy to get Friday properly underway.

Pull up a seat, grab your favourite drink and settle in — there's nostalgia, laughs, banter and plenty of tunes waiting for you. 🍹🎶

🚂 Saturday nights

🚂 The Music Train with Mr Phoenix

🕢 Live from 7:30pm

All aboard! Mr Phoenix drives the Saturday night Music Train through the decades with big tunes, nostalgia, surprises and plenty of Live Wire banter. 🚂🎵

Expect a few guest-box antics, plenty of laughs and that unmistakable Phoenix personality. One thing's for sure — you never quite know what station this train is stopping at next! 😂🔥
